Audit Services

What’s included

Tax audit
  • Tax Audit under Section 44AB
  • Form 3CA / 3CB and 3CD preparation
  • Books review and tax audit report filing
GST audit
  • GST Audit and reconciliation (GSTR-1 vs 3B vs 2B)
  • GSTR-9 / GSTR-9C annual return support
  • Input tax credit review
Statutory & company audit
  • Statutory Audit support for companies and LLPs
  • Audit coordination with auditors
  • ROC annual filing after audit
Internal & compliance audit
  • Internal Audit and controls review
  • Payroll, PF, ESI and TDS compliance review
  • Audit and notice reply support
Documents you’ll need

Typical document checklist

  • PAN of the proprietor / entity / individual
  • Aadhaar and address proof
  • Business or entity proof (if applicable)
  • Bank account details
  • Mobile number and email linked to the account
  • Previous filings, registrations or notices (if any)

The exact checklist depends on your case — your consultant confirms it when you get in touch.
